# Product Brief: Business Landing Page

**Project:** Website | **Author:** Thala | **Date:** 2026-11-10 | **Status:** Draft

## 1. Executive Summary
A fast, responsive, single-page business website that presents the company's services, builds trust through social proof, and converts visitors into leads via a clear call-to-action and contact form. Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS and hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## 2. Problem Statement
The business lacks an owned online presence. Prospects cannot quickly learn what the business offers, verify credibility, or get in touch, so leads are lost to competitors with a visible web presence.

## 3. Target Users
| Persona | Need |
|---|---|
| Prospective customer | Understand services in < 10 seconds; contact easily |
| Returning customer | Find contact info / hours quickly |
| Business owner (maintainer) | Update content without a build toolchain |

## 4. Proposed Solution
- Hero section with value proposition and primary CTA
- Services overview, About, Testimonials, FAQ, Contact
- Contact form via a static-friendly form service (e.g., Formspree)
- Deployed automatically from the `main` branch to GitHub Pages

## 5. Success Metrics
- Lighthouse Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO >= 90
- First Contentful Paint < 1.5 s on 4G
- >= 3% of visitors submit the contact form or click the CTA
- Hosting cost: $0/month

## 6. Scope
**In (MVP):** single landing page, responsive layout, contact form, SEO meta, GitHub Pages deploy, optional custom domain.
**Out:** CMS, e-commerce, user accounts, blog, multi-language.

## 7. Constraints & Assumptions
- Static hosting only (no server-side code)
- Content (copy, logo, images) provided by the business owner
- Repo hosted on GitHub (public, or private with a paid plan)
